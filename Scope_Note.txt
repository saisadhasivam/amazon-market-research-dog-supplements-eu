PROJECT: Amazon Market Research & Competitive Analysis - European Dog Supplements/Longevity Brand
DOCUMENT: Scope Note
LAST UPDATED: 15/08/2026

===========================================================
SCOPE NOTE
===========================================================

This research supports the exploratory launch of a premium dog
health, longevity, and wellness supplement brand in Europe, with
Germany and Amazon.de as the initial test market and beachhead,
followed by expansion across major European markets.

The output is a market/business-positioning study to inform
product development and launch decisions before any product is
built - it is not a technical formulation or veterinary science
document.

===========================================================
GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE
===========================================================

Primary market (deep dive): Germany

Secondary markets (sized, not deep-dived): France, United
Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Austria, Switzerland, and
other significant European markets where relevant.

===========================================================
CATEGORY SCOPE
===========================================================

In scope: dog health, wellness, longevity/healthspan, and
nutraceutical supplements only, across all formats (soft chews,
tablets, capsules, powders, liquids/oils, functional treats) and
all functional categories (joint/mobility, gut/digestive,
probiotics/prebiotics, skin/coat, omega-3, multivitamins,
immunity, calming/stress, dental, heart/cardiovascular,
cognitive/brain, senior support, healthy aging, longevity/
healthspan, antioxidants, metabolic health, weight management).

Out of scope: dog food, treats without a functional health claim,
toys, accessories, and non-dog pet categories.

Longevity/healthspan is flagged as a priority sub-focus per the
client brief - specifically whether it can become a major category
in Europe, drawing on ingredient and consumer-trend signals from
the US, Japan, and other advanced pet markets.

===========================================================
RESEARCH QUESTIONS
===========================================================

1. What is the current size, growth rate, and structure of the
   dog supplement/nutraceutical market across Germany and the
   other target European markets?
2. Who are the leading competitors on Amazon.de and in the wider
   German/European market, and what does their product,
   pricing, and positioning look like?
3. What do customers actually want - and what are they not
   getting from existing products (via review mining)?
4. Which product categories show high demand but weak
   competition, and which formats and price bands are most
   viable for a new premium entrant?
5. Is longevity/healthy aging a credible, distinct category
   opportunity in Europe, or still too early?
6. Given all of the above, which 3-5 products should a new
   premium brand launch first, in what order, and why?

===========================================================
ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK (OPPORTUNITY MATRIX)
===========================================================

Each candidate product category will be scored/ranked against the
following criteria before any launch recommendation is made:

1. Market demand
2. Growth trajectory
3. Level of competition
4. Average selling price
5. Margin potential
6. Differentiation potential
7. Repeat-purchase potential
8. Regulatory complexity
9. Amazon opportunity
10. DTC opportunity
11. Long-term brand potential

This framework is fixed before deep-dive research begins, so the
final 3-5 product recommendations are traceable to explicit,
consistently-applied criteria rather than subjective preference.

===========================================================
SOURCING STANDARDS
===========================================================

All findings are drawn from publicly available, credible sources:
company/brand websites, Amazon.de listings, professional Amazon
research tools (Helium 10, Jungle Scout, Keepa, or equivalent),
Google Trends/keyword tools, EU/German regulatory sources, and
reputable market research or news publications.

Verified data (e.g., directly observed Amazon listing data) will
be clearly distinguished from estimates (e.g., modeled market
size or sales figures) throughout. No classified, leaked, or
non-public information is used or sought. No unsupported or
generic AI-generated market-size figures are used without a
traceable source or stated estimation methodology.
